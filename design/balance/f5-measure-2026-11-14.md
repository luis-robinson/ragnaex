## What the data says
- D1: the Guardião ascension is part of it but not most of it. Removing it lowers the ratio -0.195 (cruzado 1.499 -> 1.304) and -0.093 (templario 1.336 -> 1.243); both are noticeably larger than SE. Cruzado with a clean tank is still +0.10 over 1.203 (above the 1.233 cap); templario with a clean tank is at 1.243, only +0.01 over the cap (within noise). So D1 = "measure with a non-paladin tank" shrinks the problem a lot: templario may need almost nothing, cruzado still needs a cut of about g = 0.67. Caveat: base arms also changed (cruzado base 62.5 -> 58.7 with the druid tank), so the two tanks are not a pure single-variable change; compare the asc time too (41.7 -> 45.0).
- D2: stage 2 is not uniformly "ratio goes up". fogo 1.399 -> 1.715 (+0.32, SE .09, large but noisy: base fights 100 s, near berserk), raio 1.272 -> 1.456 (+0.18, SE .034, clear), gelo 1.305 -> 1.263 (no change, within noise; gelo's caster DPS did not rise). If D2 = "count Ancestral", fogo and raio get much further over 1.203 and ELEM_STAGE_MULT[2] (C) is a real knob for them; gelo is unaffected.
- D4: the probe cannot isolate bolt vs heal (no per-skill damage dealt; --why only reports damage taken). What it shows: the ratio is entirely a damage effect: kills are 80/80, deaths ~0.1, summon DPS is flat, caster DPS +37%; the heal cannot matter for fight time here since nobody dies. So the 1.26 comes from the damage side of the Pássaro (bolt 7.5 casts/fight + form buffs); to split bolt from the rest of bird form needs an edited ANC_BOLT_POWER arm (not done, no edits allowed). The feather heal only shows up in survival/cura_tq metrics, so separating `xama_heal` (D4) is safe as far as the F5 time is concerned.
