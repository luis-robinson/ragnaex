## 1. Overview

A boss is a monster with `MonsterDef.is_boss = true`. It fights in phases driven by its HP,
summons adds, answers players who fight from range, and resets if the group loses it.
There are three kinds, and each has its own difficulty target (enforced by `boss_probe --bands`):

| Kind | Examples | Solo win | Trio win |
|---|---|---|---|
| Field | Crab King, Slime King, Boar Brute, Boar King | 60-100% | 90-100% |
| Elite (no phases) | Trent, Elven Warden | 60-100% | 90-100% |
| Group | the other 17 (dungeon, fort and citadel bosses, the three elf captains) | 20-40% | 75-100% in 30-150 s |

**Pillar (confirmed by the author):** group bosses are group content. Solo is possible but
hard, and the 20-40% solo band is a requirement, not just where tuning landed.

## 2. Detailed Design

### 2.1 Phases
`MonsterDef.phases` is a list in HP order. Each entry has `hp` (0-1 threshold) and any of:
`say` (a line shown to players on the map), `skills` (added to the boss), `summon`
(`{id, count, max, pick}`), `ward` (0-1 damage cut), `enrage` (damage multiplier).
`update_boss` runs every tick. A big hit can cross several thresholds in one tick, and then
every crossed phase applies in order. `intro` is said once per fight on first aggro, and
`last_words` on death.

### 2.2 Ward (priority-target rule)
A phase with `ward` sets a damage cut that is active only while at least one add owned by
the boss is alive. Alive adds are re-counted every `BOSS_WARD_CHECK` (0.5 s). The ward shows
as the `boss_ward` buff. Ward values are 0.4-0.5 in current content.

### 2.3 Berserk (anti-stall, confirmed by the author)
After `berserk_after` seconds in a fight (0 = never; currently 100-230 s) the boss gets
`boss_berserk` and its damage is multiplied by `BOSS_BERSERK_MULT`. It stacks with the
phase enrage. It exists mainly to stop long safe solo or kiting fights; a trio normally
finishes in 30-70 s and rarely sees it.

### 2.4 Answers to ranged play
Confirmed intent: kiting is **not free but still possible** for a skilled player.
- `SkillDef.mob_pick` (`far`, `random`, `soft`) makes a boss skill target someone other than
  the aggro target. The aggro stays on the tank. `SkillsCatalog.BOSS_PICK` turns it on for existing skills.
- A targeted boss skill follows its victim at impact (`follow_victim`, up to `BOSS_FOLLOW`).
- `barrage` places telegraphed ground circles (`m["hazards"]`) that land after a delay.
  Bots leave them.
- `pull` is a hook that drags a target next to the boss. Bosses cannot be pulled.
- Boss summon skills and boss BUFF skills ignore range (`_skill_reach`), so adds come on cooldown.

### 2.5 Escort, reset, rewards
- A boss with `escort` spawns followers that respawn while the leader is alive and out of combat.
- A boss that is out of combat while hurt (or past phase 0) walks home. On arrival `reset_boss`
  restores HP, phase, berserk, ward, threat, cooldowns, and removes its hazards.
- Gold is x3, legendary coins use `LEGENDARY_COINS_BOSS` (or the monster's own `coin_*`),
  gear drop uses `GEAR_DROP_CHANCE_BOSS`. Adds from `summon` give 0 XP.

## 3. Formulas

| Name | Formula | Variables / range | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ward damage | `taken = max(1, round(amount × (1 − ward)))` while `adds_alive > 0` | `ward` 0.4-0.5 | 1000 hit, ward 0.5 → 500 |
| Boss damage multiplier | `dmg_mult = enrage_mult × (BOSS_BERSERK_MULT if berserk else 1)` | `enrage_mult` 1.0-1.35, `BOSS_BERSERK_MULT` 1.6 | enrage 1.3 + berserk → 2.08 |
| Phase trigger | `hp / max_hp ≤ phase.hp` | `phase.hp` 0.15-0.7 | 3-phase boss: 0.7, 0.4, 0.15 |
| Victim pool | characters within `BOSS_PICK_RANGE` (16 m), not stealthed, not perched; characters preferred over summons | | |
| Barrage spots | 1 at the aim, 1 per other victim (if apart by more than `radius`), rest random within `spread` (8 m) of the boss | `count` default 3 | |
| Targeted skill follow | impact point moves to the nearest enemy within `BOSS_FOLLOW` (5 m) of the aim | | |

Per-boss HP, ATK and cooldowns are catalog values, not formulas (`monsters_catalog.gd`).

## 4. Edge Cases

- **Several thresholds in one hit:** all crossed phases apply in the same tick, in order.
- **Stealthed or perched victims:** never picked by `mob_pick`.
- **No victim in range:** when `pick_victim` finds nobody (null), the boss casts the skill at its aggro target as usual (`monster_brain.gd`). Summoned adds with `pick` fall back to the boss's aggro target the same way.
- **Pull on a boss:** ignored.
- **Boss dies or leaves the map with circles pending:** its hazards are removed without landing.
- **Reset:** hazards owned by the boss are removed, `adds_epoch` increments so old adds stop counting, cooldowns clear.
- **Kiter shooting from range:** does not trigger a reset; only losing combat for `AGGRO_LOSE_TIME` does.
- **Adds with `boss_id`:** they never drop XP and are not counted as a player-summon.
- **Fight that never started in the probe:** a boss at full HP with no target stays idle; the probe bot walks back (probe-only behaviour, not game behaviour).

## 5. Dependencies

| System | Why | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| Combat / damage (`_deal_damage`, `Combat.calc_damage`) | ward and berserk modify damage | bosses → combat |
| Skill pipeline (`MonsterBrain`, `_apply_effects`) | boss skills, `barrage`, `pull`, `mob_pick` | bosses ↔ skills |
| Threat / aggro | victim picking leaves aggro on the tank | bosses ← threat |
| Loot (`LootSystem`) | boss drop rules | bosses → loot |
| Party | trio is the calibration group; shared kill credit | bosses ← party |
| Raid / fort assaults | captains march with raids | bosses ↔ raid |
| Bots (`BotBrain`) | dodge hazards; probe uses `StayBrain` | bosses → bots |
| Maps / spawners | boss spawners, escorts, dungeon seals | bosses ← maps |
| `Balance` | all `BOSS_*` constants | bosses ← balance |
| `Events` | `hazard_placed`, `notification` for lines | bosses → presentation |

Dependencies are bidirectional in code; the other systems' GDDs (not written yet) must mention bosses.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every boss in `boss_probe.BOSSES` is in its band under `--bands --trials=20` (confirm solo with 40).
- [ ] Group bosses: solo win 20-40%, trio 75-100% in 30-150 s, 4+ casts/min, 35%+ time acting, 4+ adds, 10%+ off-target damage.
- [ ] A kiter at 9.5 m+ takes at least 2 boss skills per minute, or loses the fight.
- [ ] While an add is alive, a boss with `ward` takes `(1 − ward)` of the damage; with none alive, full damage.
- [ ] A boss past `berserk_after` deals `BOSS_BERSERK_MULT` x damage.
- [ ] A boss that loses combat returns home and resets to phase 0 at full HP, with no hazards left.
- [ ] A pull skill never moves a boss.
- [ ] Tests: `_check_boss_mechanics` and `boss_anim_probe` pass; `run_tests` 0 failed.
