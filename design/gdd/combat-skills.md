## 1. Overview

Action combat in the Drakantos style: the character faces the cursor, LMB is the basic
attack, and up to 6 equipped skills aim like a MOBA (self, lane, ground circle, cone,
target). Every action goes through the simulation as an `InputCommand`, so the player,
bots and monsters use the same pipeline. Damage is ATK against DEF with a flat
mitigation, crits and variance, then a chain of reductions (block, parry, curses,
shields). Skills are short and chainable: each skill hits once at its impact frame,
and the next action can start right after impact.

Out of scope here: the skill web and class talents (how skills are learned and
improved), bosses (`design/gdd/bosses.md`), and per-class skill content
(`data/catalog/skills_catalog.gd`).

## 2. Detailed Design

### 2.1 Action bar and learning
A skill must be learned (points in the web) and placed in one of `SKILL_SLOTS` (6) bar
slots. The bar can only be changed in town (`LOADOUT_TOWN_ONLY`). A cast fails if the
skill's unlock level, learned state, required weapon, cooldown or mana is not met.
On success the mana is spent and the cooldown starts:
`cooldown = cooldown_at(mastery) × skill_cooldown_mult` (minimum 0.2 s).

### 2.2 Cast timeline
Every skill and basic attack has three phases: `startup` (wind-up), `active`
(hit window) and `recovery`. The state is CASTING for the whole timeline.
- **Impact**: at `t ≥ startup` the skill applies once. Targets are chosen at impact, so
  anyone who left the shape or died during the wind-up is not hit.
- **Channels** spread extra pulses over the active phase.
- **Chaining** (characters only): another skill or attack can start
  `CHAIN_AFTER_IMPACT` (0.05 s) after impact. Basic attack into basic attack does not chain.
- **Movement cancel**: only after impact, at `startup + active + min(cancel_after, CANCEL_AFTER_MAX)`.
- **Dash cancel**: a dash cancels any cast. Before impact, mana and cooldown are refunded.
- **Interrupt (confirmed intent)**: a stun during the cast loses the skill, with no
  refund. Getting interrupted costs the cast; only a deliberate dash cancel is forgiven.
- **Input buffer**: a command pressed during a cast is kept for `INPUT_BUFFER_TICKS`
  (0.25 s) and the buffer does not count down while your own cast runs.
- **Movement while casting**: ×`CAST_MOVE_MULT` (0.6); ×0.25 when `startup ≥ 0.45`;
  0 for dash skills. Facing locks until impact when `lock_facing` is set.
- **Hit-stop**: frames from the skill (basic attack: `HIT_STOP_FRAMES_BASIC`), only
  when something was hit and the player is involved.
- No casting in the air: actions wait for landing (buffered).

### 2.3 Targeting
HEAL and BUFF skills are friendly; everything else targets hostiles. A target must be
alive and not a non-combat NPC. Results are cut to `max_targets`.

| Mode | Shape |
|---|---|
| SELF | the caster; with `radius`, everyone valid within it |
| DIRECTION | a lane of length `range` and half-width `radius` from the caster |
| GROUND | a circle of `radius` at the aim, aim clamped to `range` |
| CONE | within `radius`, inside `cone_angle_deg` |
| TARGET | the valid entity nearest the cursor (within 1.6 m) and within `range`; friendly skills fall back to self; `radius` splashes |

The target's own radius is added to every distance test.

### 2.4 Basic attack
Built per class and weapon (`game_data.gd`). Cooldown is the class value divided by
`(1 + attack_speed/100)`. No mana.

| Weapon | Shape |
|---|---|
| Melee (default) | cone 110°, class range, 2 targets |
| Spear | 3.3 m, 40°, 3 targets, power ×1.15, cooldown ×1.1 |
| Two-handed big swing | reach ×1.4, 150°, 4 targets |
| Ranged | projectile, speed 22, 1 target |
| Rifle | cooldown ×1.8, power ×1.9, range ×1.2, speed 40 |

### 2.5 Effects
Supported by `_apply_effects` / `_apply_skill`: `dot` (and bleed with `atk_pct`), `slow`,
`root`, `stun`, `curse`, `wither`, `pull`, `heal_pct`, `buff`, `shield`, `dash`, `summon`,
`taunt`, `heal_pct_of_dmg`, `mend_pct_of_dmg`, `combust`, `bounce`, `aura` (toggle),
`trap`, `pet_command`, `barrage`, `combo_spend`, `form`, `stealth`. Nothing applies to a
dead target. Buffs and shields never land on enemies (they go to the caster instead).

### 2.6 Status rules
- Buffs, debuffs, curses and bleeds with an id **renew** (newest duration), never stack.
- Slows, roots, wither and plain dots **stack** across casts (confirmed intent: group
  crowd control is meant to pile up). Slows multiply, with a floor of 10% speed.
- Any root blocks movement, jump and dash.
- Stun: default 1 s, ×0.4 on bosses; a new stun overwrites the current one. Non-combat
  NPCs are immune. Storm skills have 15% chance of a 0.5 s stun on a direct hit.
- Fire skills' dots are burns (`fire:true`, set when the catalog loads); frost skills deal
  +30% to slowed, rooted or stunned targets (shatter).

### 2.7 Guard and block
- **Guard arc** `BLOCK_ARC_DEG` (140°) in front. The hit's origin is the attacker, the
  charge start, the ground impact or the projectile's side.
- **Active guard** (hold RMB): always blocks hits inside the arc unless stunned.
  Reduction = `block_reduction + GUARD_HOLD_REDUCTION_BONUS` (+10 with a shield), cap 85%.
  Costs 2 MP/s, halves movement, stops mana regen. At 0 MP the guard breaks and returns
  only after releasing with MP ≥ 5.
- **Passive block** (shield only): chance `block_chance` for `block_reduction`.
- **Parry** (shield only): raising the guard opens a 0.22 s window (0.9 s cooldown).
  A parried hit takes ×(1 − `PARRY_REDUCTION`) **after** the block reduction. Confirmed
  intent: the two compound, so a perfect parry is near-immune (about 98.5%).
- Shield reactions on block (stun on parry, Oath and Grace) are in `ShieldSystem`.

### 2.8 Dash
A dash moves the character and cancels casts (with refund before impact). Today it has
**no invulnerability**: the character can be hit mid-dash.
**Design gap (confirmed by the author): dash should have invulnerability frames.**
The length of the window is not designed yet.

### 2.9 Threat
Threat = damage × (1 + `threat_pct`/100). A monster switches target when another source
passes 110% of the current target's threat, unless taunted. Taunt puts the taunter at
top threat ×1.1 + 1 for `TAUNT_DURATION` (4 s, half on bosses). Aggro drops after
`AGGRO_LOSE_TIME` (6 s) without combat.

## 3. Formulas

### 3.1 Skill power
`power = power_at(mastery) × skill_power_mult(set) × element_mult × shield_power × power_scale`
then, when they apply: × `AMBUSH_MULT` (1.6, from stealth), × combo spend
`(1 + pct/100 × stacks)`, × `FROST_SHATTER_MULT` (1.3), × legendary power.
Mastery fallback per level: +12% power, −4% mana, −5% cooldown.

### 3.2 Damage (`Combat.calc_damage`)
```
atk, def, crit = (matk, mdef, crit_mag) if magical else (patk, pdef, crit_phys)
if behind and backstab > 0: crit += BACKSTAB_CRIT (25); power ×= 1 + backstab/100
raw    = max(atk × power − def × DEF_FACTOR, atk × power × 0.15)
amount = raw × (1 ± DMG_VARIANCE) × (CRIT_MULT if crit else 1)
if blocked: amount ×= 1 − min(reduction, 85)/100   (a blocked hit cannot crit)
result = max(1, round(amount))
```
`DEF_FACTOR` 0.5, `DMG_VARIANCE` 0.1, `CRIT_MULT` 1.5.
Example: ATK 200, power 1.5, DEF 80 → raw = 300 − 40 = 260; crit → 390; active guard
with 55% reduction → 175.

### 3.3 After the roll (`_deal_damage`, in order)
1. Parry ×0.1, or a monster's `front_guard` (physical from the front).
2. Class-talent damage multiplier (not on dots).
3. Curses: ×(1 + taken%) × (1 − min(weaken, 50)%).
4. Boss ward ×(1 − ward).
5. Damage reduction ×(1 − min(dr_pct, `DR_CAP` 40)%).
6. Evasion roll (characters, not dots): full dodge.
7. Shield HP absorbs, then mana shield.
8. HP loss; lifesteal on direct hits.

### 3.4 Healing
`heal = matk × power × heal_power × (1 ± 0.08) × crit`, then × the target's `heal_factor`
(wither cuts it, default 50% for 5 s).

### 3.5 Attributes → stats (per `ATTR_SCALE` = 10 points)
| Attribute | Effect per 10 points |
|---|---|
| FOR | physical ATK ×2.0 (bow/rifle ×0.3, spear ×1.0, dagger ×1.2); block chance 12 + 0.6×max(FOR, WILL) with a shield; block reduction 30 + 0.5×FOR (cap 60) |
| AGI | physical ATK for bow/rifle ×2.2, spear ×1.6, dagger ×1.0, others ×0.25; crit 5 + 0.4×AGI |
| VIT | HP ×12, physical DEF ×1.5 |
| WILL | MP ×8, magic DEF ×1.5, heal power +3% |
| INT | magic ATK ×2.5, magic crit 5 + 0.4×INT |

Base: patk += 4 + 0.8×level; matk = gear + 2.5×INT + 4 + 0.8×level. Off-hand weapon ATK
counts ×0.6. Caps: lifesteal 20, attack speed 60, buff duration 80, radiance 40,
evasion 45, cooldown reduction 25, element 60.

## 4. Edge Cases

- **Target leaves or dies during the wind-up:** not hit (targets are chosen at impact).
- **Caster stunned mid-cast:** the skill is lost, no refund (confirmed intent).
- **Dash before impact:** cast cancelled, mana and cooldown refunded.
- **Hit while dashing:** takes full damage today (gap: should have i-frames).
- **Friendly fire:** none; characters are never hostile to each other.
- **Projectile:** removed when its owner is gone, at max range, outside the map or on
  solid geometry (perched owners ignore walls); each target is hit once.
- **Bouncing projectile:** re-targets if its target dies in flight.
- **Guard at 0 mana:** breaks; needs release and MP ≥ 5 to raise again.
- **Hit from behind while guarding:** outside the 140° arc, so no block.
- **Mounted when hit:** dismounted.
- **Perched NPC:** cannot be damaged.
- **Stacked slows:** never below 10% speed.
- `State.ATTACKING` exists but the sim never sets it (casts and basics both use CASTING).

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `calc_damage` matches §3.2 for physical and magical hits, with and without crit and block (`run_tests` checks).
- [ ] A skill hits only targets inside its shape at impact time.
- [ ] Dash before impact refunds mana and cooldown; a stun before impact does not.
- [ ] Chaining: a skill can start 0.05 s after the previous impact; basic into basic does not chain.
- [ ] Two slows on one target multiply; speed never drops below 10%.
- [ ] Active guard blocks every hit inside the 140° arc; a hit from behind is not blocked.
- [ ] Parry inside the 0.22 s window cuts the hit to 10% of the blocked amount.
- [ ] `combat_probe` reports 0 FLAG (latency, chaining, buffer, dash cancel, impact on the damage tick).
- [ ] `skill_audit` reports no `WEAK` skill below 60% of the median.
- [ ] (Future) A dash grants invulnerability for its i-frame window.
