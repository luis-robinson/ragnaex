## 1. Overview

Slow, build-focused progression from level 1 to 30 (about 15 h of planned play). Each
level gives points to spend in a Path of Exile–style **skill web** per class: skills,
their mastery and evolutions, attributes and notable bonuses. Every 5 levels the
character also picks one of three **class talents**. Rebuilding is free in town, so
experimenting with builds costs a walk, not gold.

Out of scope: damage and attribute formulas (`design/gdd/combat-skills.md`), bosses
(`design/gdd/bosses.md`), gear progression (tiers, legendaries, enhancement).

## 2. Detailed Design

### 2.1 Levels and XP
- Max level `MAX_LEVEL` 30. At 30, XP stops; reaching 30 sets XP to 0.
- XP per level comes from planned playtime (see §3.1): about 2 h for 1–10, 5 h for 10–20,
  8 h for 20–30 of pure hunting. Level-up restores 30% of HP and MP.
- **Kill XP** goes to the top damager (the party's damage counts together) and is shared with
  party members who are alive, on the same map, within 45 m and within 15 levels.
- **Quests** fill a set share of each band: 50% (1–10), 55% (10–20), 65% (20–30). The XP
  written in a quest is a weight; `QuestsCatalog.scale_xp` scales them to the band share.
  Confirmed intent: questing is meant to be faster than the pure-hunting time targets.
- Other XP: repeatable hunts pay monster XP ×10 ×1.6; elites ×6; raid levies +8% per step.
- **Death** costs `DEATH_XP_PENALTY` (3%) of the XP inside the current level, player only;
  it never drops a level.

### 2.2 Skill web
- **Points**: 8 per level, 9 at levels 10, 20 and 30 (`points_at`): 235 at level 30.
- **Buying**: a node can be bought when it links to a bought node, the character meets its
  level, and has the points. Everything goes through `tree_allocate`.
- **Layout** (per class, fixed seed `hash(class + "_teia")`): a grid of irregular cells
  (`CELL` 4.6), one sector for general skills plus one per build, by angle. Skills are placed
  by unlock level, so higher-level skills sit farther from the start.
  - **Junctions** (cell corners) and **roads** (a random spanning tree plus 55% of the other
    edges, `EDGE_KEEP`): each is a 1-point node with +1 of the sector's attribute.
  - **Skill circle**: the skill (1 point, its unlock level) with satellites: an entry node
    (+5..+10 of the build attribute by depth), the mastery node, the skill's evolutions, and
    build passives (role stat ×0.25) in the free slots.
  - **Notable ring** (every 3rd cell): 5 ring nodes (3 × +5..+10 attribute, 2 × +1) and a
    centre with a bonus ×0.2 (`NOTABLE_SCALE`) from `ARM_NOTABLES` (by role) or
    `RING_NOTABLES`, plus +6..+12 of the main attribute; two entrances.
  - The first general cell holds the dash evolutions, chained from the start.
  - 200–480 nodes and at least 40 attribute nodes per class (tested).
- **Bar**: a learned skill goes to the first free bar slot; the bar changes only in town.

### 2.3 Mastery
Levels 0–5 (`MASTERY_MAX`), 1 point each. Values come from the skill's `mastery` array
(`power_mult`, `mp_mult`, `cd_mult`, `extra`); without one, each level gives +12% power,
−4% mana, −5% cooldown. Each level also gives +10% to the skill's positive buff and form
bonuses (`MASTERY_BONUS_PCT`).

### 2.4 Evolutions
46 `TalentDef`s (one dash evolution per class at level 5; 5–7 per class in total). Each
changes one skill or the dash (`set`/`mult`/`effects`), costs its own points, sits in the
skill's circle, and only works with its required weapon equipped.

### 2.5 Class talents
6 rows (levels 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30) × 3 options per class, one pick per row (144 options
for 8 classes). Picks cost 0, need no neighbour, are level-gated, and swap for free only in
town (`PICK_SWAP_TOWN_ONLY`, `pick_sibling`). Hook types:

| Hook | Effect |
|---|---|
| `permanent` | a skill's positive buff always on (× strength) |
| `aura` | the same on you, party members and your summons within 15 m |
| `summon_permanent` / `summon_extra` | summons never expire / higher summon cap |
| `skill_mods` | changes a skill like an evolution (`duration` scales effect time) |
| `stats` | flat stats |
| `form_mult` / `form_time` | stronger / longer forms |
| `cheat_death` / `ally_save` | a fatal hit leaves you (or a nearby ally) at 1 HP, immortal briefly, on a cooldown |
| `ambush_mult` | damage of the hit that breaks stealth |
| `on_kill` | heal %, mana %, stealth, or refresh a buff on kill |
| `low_hp` / `execute` | more damage while you are low / against low targets |
| `regen` | % HP per second (optionally only in form or as an aura) |
| `mana_shield` | part of incoming damage paid from mana |
| `trap_max` / `oath_max` | higher trap / Oath-charge caps |

### 2.6 Respec (confirmed intent: free, town only)
`reset_tree` near `SKILL_RESET_NPCS` (Elder, High Priest) clears the web, mastery and
evolutions and refunds all points. Start node, starting skills and class talents are kept.
Bar slots with unlearned skills are emptied. No per-node refund.

### 2.7 Bots
On level-up bots pick class talents (preferring ones that mention their build's skills),
buy the shortest path to each build skill in order, raise build mastery up to level ÷ 6,
buy each available evolution with a 50% chance, then spend the rest greedily on notables
and attributes of their own branch.

## 3. Formulas

### 3.1 XP to the next level
```
minutes(lv) = XP_MIN_BASE + XP_MIN_PER_LEVEL × (lv − 1)          = 6.5 + 1.75 × (lv − 1)
mob_xp(lv)  = XP_MOB_BASE + XP_MOB_PER_LEVEL × lv                = 6 + 4.5 × lv
xp_for_level(lv) = round10( minutes × XP_KILLS_PER_MIN × mob_xp ÷ (1 − XP_QUEST_SHARE) )
                 = round10( minutes × 3 × mob_xp ÷ 0.55 ),  0 at level 30
```
Example: level 10 → minutes 22.25, mob_xp 51 → 22.25 × 3 × 51 ÷ 0.55 ≈ 6190.
The curve uses the flat 0.45 quest share while quests fill 0.50–0.65 of each band, so a
player who quests levels faster than the minutes above (confirmed intent).

### 3.2 Kill XP
```
each      = mob.xp × (1 + PARTY_XP_BONUS × (n − 1)) ÷ n        PARTY_XP_BONUS = 0.15, n = sharers
gained(c) = int(each × clamp(1 + 0.08 × (mob.level − c.level), 0.3, 1.6))
```
Example: 3 players, mob XP 100, all same level → each 100 × 1.3 ÷ 3 ≈ 43.

### 3.3 Quest XP
`k(band) = quest_share(band) × band_xp(band) ÷ Σ written_xp(band)`; each quest pays
`round10(written_xp × k)`. Non-repeatable quests only.

### 3.4 Points
`points_at(lv) = 8 (+1 at 10, 20, 30)`; total at 30 = 235.

### 3.5 Death
`xp −= int(xp × 0.03)` (XP inside the current level).

## 4. Edge Cases

- **XP at level 30:** ignored.
- **Talent or node above your level:** refused ("requer nível X").
- **Mastery at 5:** refused ("maestria já no máximo").
- **Respec with skills on the bar:** slots holding unlearned skills are emptied.
- **Old saves:** saves without a web get nodes marked from learned skills and evolutions;
  `save_version < 4` or a node that no longer exists resets the web and refunds all points
  with a notice; the bar is rebuilt from learned skills.
- **Party member too far or too many levels apart:** gets no XP from the kill.
- **Level difference:** a mob far above you gives at most ×1.6; far below, at least ×0.3.
- **Quick-start presets** grant the points for their level.
- `Balance.mastery_cost` exists but is unused (mastery is 1 point per level).

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Planned time to 30 is 13–17 h (`_check_xp_budget`).
- [ ] Quests give each band's share ±5% and there are 10+ quests per band (`_check_xp_budget`).
- [ ] Every level 2–19 gets at least 35% of its XP from quests (`_check_xp_budget`).
- [ ] Level 25 needs 100–400 skeleton kills (`_check_xp_budget`).
- [ ] Each class web has 200–480 nodes and 40+ attribute nodes (`run_tests`).
- [ ] A node can only be bought when it links to a bought node and the level is met.
- [ ] Respec works only near the Elder or High Priest, refunds every point and keeps class talents.
- [ ] **(New requirement, confirmed by the author)** At level 30, a full web gives about
      ×1.4 ATK and ×1.2 HP over no web, for every build (`web_probe`), enforced by a test
      with a tolerance to be set.
